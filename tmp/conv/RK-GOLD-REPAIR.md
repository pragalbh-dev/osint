**Hand:** GOLD · **Worktree:** `wt-GOLD-REPAIR` · **Branch:** `bakeoff/gold-repair` · **Date:** 2026-07-26

**The discipline, stated up front.** Every change below is justified against the **document**. I did

authoring these repairs; the only inputs were the seven corpus documents and the gold's own declared
labelling conventions. Where the honest repair makes a surface *harder* to match, it was still made
(two of them do). Where a change would have raised scores on a contestable reading, it was not made,
and it is named in *Left alone* with the reason.

## 1. The 16 non-verbatim rows

16 of 65 scored rows carried a role surface appearing nowhere in their cited document, so no verbatim
extractor could produce them and absolute recall was capped for everyone at 49/65. I went through all
16 individually. **The split: 2 transcription slips, 12 annotator compositions with a recoverable
faithful label, 2 that had to leave the scored set.** Nothing was deleted.

### (a) Transcription slips — the surface IS in the document, slightly altered — 2 rows

| row | was | now | the document |
|---|---|---|---|
| `d02-r11` | `the exact number of…` | `The exact number of batteries/launchers (TELs) inducted` | d02 L17 opens the sentence with it; the annotator lowercased a sentence-initial capital |
| `d19-r07` | `a commercial ELINT aggregator feed` | `A commercial ELINT aggregator feed` | d19 L13, same slip |

`d02-r11` is worth flagging: it is a `known_gap`, and the bake-off diagnosis argued that type is
"unnameable by construction — a gap is described, not named". Not here. d02 states the withheld
quantity in its own voice and names it exactly. One character of case was the whole defect.

### (b) Annotator compositions — a faithful label was recoverable — 12 rows

Five sub-kinds, all repaired the same way: replace the composed string with the document's own surface
for that role. The detail the composition carried was already held in `tier3_attributes` in every case,
so nothing was lost.

**Ellipsis restored** (the annotator elided document text with `...`). Both repairs make the surface
**longer**, which is the clearest possible evidence this is not matcher-fitting.
- `d04-r05` `a longer-ranged variant ... the HQ-9BE` → the contiguous d04 L9 span.
- `d19-r11` `a PAF/Army Air Defence Command HQ-9BE ... battery` → the contiguous d19 L15 span, parenthetical included.

**Annotator locator stripped.** The gold's own `mention_verbatim_note` already rules that a trailing
parenthetical locator is *annotation, not document text*, and that "any scorer doing exact-match
against document spans MUST strip a trailing parenthetical before comparing". That rule was applied to
the 120 coref mentions and never to claim role surfaces. Applying it is not a new leniency; it is the
gold's existing convention finally applied consistently.
- `d17b-r03` `… access spur (the suspected forward site)` → `any of the hardstands or along the access spur`
- `d17b-r04` `The cleared laydown area (the suspected forward site)` → `The cleared laydown area`
- `d20-r15` `@faisal_defencewatch (Post 1)` → `@faisal_defencewatch` — `(Post 1)` is literally the form the note names.

**Fusion / appended resolution undone** (two document spans glued by an annotator comma, or the
resolved parent place appended).
- `d19-r02` `Rahwali airfield, in the northeast dispersal area` → `Rahwali airfield`. Two separate surfaces in one d19 L11 sentence. The place role is the site, which the gold already declares as a `basing_site` at `d19-r01`; the sub-area stays in `tier3_attributes`.
- `d19-r05` `…east of the TEL cluster, Rahwali airfield` → `its prepared hardstand roughly 380 m east of the TEL cluster`. The leading portion was already verbatim.
- `cs01-r09` `the HQ-9/P battery` → `the HQ-9/P`. cs01 L11 describes "Battery composition" of the system it names as `the HQ-9/P`.

**Slash alternation collapsed** (the annotator offered two surfaces for one role).
- `cs01-r07` `The missile itself / the HQ-9/P` → `The missile itself`, the subject of the cited L17 sentence. The identity to `the HQ-9/P` is carried by coref cluster `cs01-C1-hq9p`, which is where it belongs — not by a slash inside a role surface.

**Coordination distributed by the annotator.**
- `d19-r14`, `d19-r16` `Sialkot-area dispersal site` → `Sialkot-area`. d19 L17 names the pair once — "the previously tracked Sialkot-area and Pasrur dispersal sites" — with the head noun shared across the coordination, so no contiguous span reads "Sialkot-area dispersal site". The site type is already carried by the entity type (`basing_site`) and `tier3_attributes.site_type`. The sibling `d19-r15` needed no repair only because it happens to sit at the end of the coordination.

`d19-r14` is the row the diagnosis found the harness scoring as a **correct gold match** and a
**fabrication** simultaneously, in the same run, for both candidates. That contradiction was the gold's
non-verbatim surface, and it is now gone.

**Quoted proposition replaced by its entity head.**
- `d17b-r12` subject was the entire reported assertion `"fresh HQ-9/P TEL deployment confirmed near port road site, imagery pending"` (and mis-transcribed it — d17b L30 has the comma *inside* the closing quote). `observed-at` relates a thing observed to a place; the document's own surfaces for those two roles, both inside the quoted post, are `fresh HQ-9/P TEL deployment` and `near port road site`. `evidence_mode` stays `stated-attributed`, so "the document reports this rather than endorsing it" is unchanged.

### (c) Genuinely not verbatim-extractable — 2 rows, moved out, named, not deleted

## 2. The anti-coreference traps were in the positive set

**Confirmed, and it is worse than "misfiled".** `coref_cluster` on a claim row is handed to the scorer
as the B³ gold clustering — an instruction that rows sharing a tag *should* share a referent. Two of
the 32 curated registry clusters are not co-reference clusters at all; they are **contrast sets**:

- `d05-C4-events` — `ANTI_COREF (distinct stated identifiers)`, annotator note: "**THE over-merge trap.**"
- `d19-C4-othersites` — `ANTI_COREF (stated enumeration)`, "They must not merge."

They were the **only two clusters in the entire slice carrying two or more entity-form claims**, so
they supplied essentially the whole positive binding denominator. The artifact was instructing a
candidate to merge the very pairs the corpus planted to catch merging, and paying it for doing so.

**Repair — a labelling correction, on the label's own terms.** An `ANTI_COREF` cluster's members do
not co-refer, so writing one shared referent tag across them was wrong as a label, independently of
any metric. Each member now carries its own row-bookkeeping tag — the mechanism `cluster_count_note`
already describes for rows outside a curated chain — and the relation rows carry a `*-contrast` tag:

`d05-r01/02/03` → `d05-C4a-gd118834` / `d05-C4b-gd118835` / `d05-C4c-gd119011`;
`d05-r04/05/06` → `d05-C4-contrast`; `d19-r14/r15` → `d19-C4a-sialkot` / `d19-C4b-pasrur`;
`d19-r16/r17` → `d19-C4-contrast`.

**The registry entries are unchanged** — they still publish the `ANTI_COREF` category, the licensing
quote, the mentions and the annotator's over-merge-trap note. Nothing was deleted; the prohibition was
moved out of the "bind these" channel.

**And the prohibition was made checkable.** Withholding the tag stops the metric *paying* for the
merge, but it does not make merging *cost* anything. Two `ANTI_COREF` negative-gold rows were added,
one per contrast set, each on the pair the document itself puts closest together:

- **`d05-r25`** `GD No: KPQA-HC-2020-118834` ⊥ `GD No: KPQA-HC-2020-118835`, cited at d05 L48 — "Container: TCNU7712204 (shared, see line 118834)". This is the trap in one line: same consignee, shipper, vessel, filing date, HS-code family *and container*, so both name similarity and shared neighbourhood argue for one event; only the distinct GD and B/L identifiers say no — and the cited line is the document naming the container as SHARED while cross-referencing the *other* line by number, which is a document that knows it has two lines.
- **`d19-r21`** `Sialkot-area` ⊥ `Pasrur dispersal site`, cited at d19 L17 — enumeration plus an explicit cardinality ("both").

Negative gold is excluded from **both** the recall and the precision denominator, so these two rows
cannot raise or lower any candidate's score. They can only catch the over-merge.

### The sweep for other misplaced negative-semantics rows

I checked every registry cluster whose `licensing_category` begins `ANTI_COREF` or `AMBIGUOUS` (both
are withholding categories; no positively-licensed cluster begins with either word).

- **`d20-AMBIG-2` — the same leak, one order down, and it was live.** Category `AMBIGUOUS` ("a retweet that changes the destination … three mutually incompatible objects, and no coordinates for any of them"), and it tagged **two** positive claims, `d20-r03` and `d20-r06`, so the scorer was again being told to bind them. Retagged to `d20-AMBIG-2a-rawalpindi` / `d20-AMBIG-2b-portroad`. Withholding the tag does **not** assert the two destinations are distinct — it takes an unresolvable pair out of a binding metric, which is the only faithful treatment of "the document does not settle this".
- **Four withholding clusters tag exactly one positive claim each** — `d04-ANTI-1` (`d04-r07`), `d19-AMBIG-1` (`d19-r09`), `d17b-AMBIG-1` (`d17b-r12`), `d20-AMBIG-1` (`d20-r04`). A singleton tag instructs no binding *between rows*, so the defect the diagnosis identified is not present. **Left as authored and reported rather than removed** — and note the direction: removing them would *raise* every candidate's B³ precision, which is not a change a gold owner should make unprompted.
- Every other curated cluster carries a positive licence and is untouched.

**Result:** no scored cluster now contains two or more entity-form claims. Which exposes the deeper
fact — see ceiling 3 below.

## 4. Coverage asymmetry — stated as a ceiling, never padded

A new `ceilings` block in `claim-gold.json` declares three ceilings. All three are measured from the
gold and the corpus alone.

**1 — the gold is curated, not exhaustive.** 65 scored rows over 3,846 words = **1.69 labeled claims
per 100 words**, and the density varies **four-fold** across the seven documents (0.66/100w in
`cs01_stale_orbat` to 2.69/100w in `d05_customs_manifest`). A gold that enumerated everything its
documents state would not vary by document type; this one is curated by analytic interest. That is a
coverage fact, not a defect.

*Consequence, stated in the artifact:* **precision is uninterpretable in absolute terms.** Its
denominator is everything the candidate emitted, and this gold does not enumerate everything the
documents license, so a grounded, correctly-cited claim over an unlabeled sentence is charged as a
false positive. Precision therefore measures how closely a candidate's output volume matches this
gold's *curation* volume — it rewards terseness, which for an OSINT extractor is the wrong direction —
and any F1 built on it inherits the defect while harmonic-meaning away genuine recall signal.
*How to report:* recall against the verbatimness ceiling; precision only as a paired diagnostic
alongside the emission count, never as a quality score, never as a ranking.

**2 — verbatim extractability.** 63/65 from the document, 44/65 from the row's own cited span. The
second is the softer ceiling on the *lexical grounding* proxies, which look inside the cited span only.

**3 — the positive coref binding denominator is empty.** No positively-licensed cluster contains two
or more entity-form claim rows. Before this repair that was *masked*: the only two clusters that did
were the ANTI_COREF pair. Fixing the sign inversion exposes that there was never a positive one. B³
over this slice cannot reward a correct positive bind between two system-indexable claims. **Report
coref binding as UNMEASURABLE ON THIS GOLD with this reason named — do not re-weight it silently to
zero, which turns an honest gap into an invisible one.** What would fix it is at least one curated
cluster where two entity-form rows genuinely co-refer; `d05-C1-consignee`, whose own note calls it
"The ONLY identifier-backed equivalence in the slice", is the natural candidate but currently carries
one entity row and one triple row. That is corpus work, to be done without reference to any
candidate's output.

**Explicitly rejected and recorded in the artifact:** padding the gold with rows until it matches what
a candidate emitted. That is labelling from the thing being graded. The honest fix is exhaustive
labelling of all seven documents by a hand that has seen no candidate output — a data investment, not
a repair.

## 5. Tempting changes I rejected

Each of these would have moved a number. None would help an analyst reading a real document.

- **Shorten the four `entity:variant` surfaces to bare designators** (`HQ-9BE` instead of `a longer-ranged variant referred to in most recent trade literature as the HQ-9BE`). Would take that bucket from 0/4 toward 4/4 in one edit. **Rejected** — labelling the mention noun phrase is the gold's stated convention, not a defect, and shortening it is rewording labels so extractors match them more easily. I did the opposite: `d04-r05` and `d19-r11` got *longer* because that is what the document says.
- **Delete `d05-r04/r05/r06`** (pairwise `distinct-from` over three GD numbers), which the diagnosis argues "any model that scored would be fabricating". **Rejected** — d05 lists three GD line items with three distinct GD numbers and three distinct B/L numbers and states a cross-reference between two of them in its own voice. Distinctness by enumeration with unique primary identifiers is about as stated as distinctness gets in a customs manifest. Deleting them would raise every candidate's recall on a contestable reading, which is precisely the direction a gold owner must not move.
- **Delete the two `d19` Sialkot rows** rather than repair their surfaces. **Rejected** — deletion raises the reported number without changing anything about extraction, and it would hide the matcher-vs-`extract_only_stated` contradiction that `d19-r14` exposes. Repairing the surface fixes the contradiction *and* keeps the row.
- **Relabel `d19-r08` as an `EVENT:SightingEvent`** so it becomes matchable. **Rejected** — the argument for it runs through what the pipeline emits, not through what the document says. That is reasoning from the thing being graded.
- **Add POST 6's stated gap as a replacement row for `d20-r14`.** **Rejected as a repair** — it is a real gap and a defensible future row, but adding rows is coverage, not repair, and it moves a denominator.
- **Split `d19-r17`** (whose subject is a coordinated pair naming two entities) into two well-formed rows. **Rejected** — same reason: it *adds* a gold row.
- **Add an `AMBIGUOUS` negative row for `d20-AMBIG-2`'s destination conflict**, which has no negative-row counterpart. **Not done** — completing the corpus is outside a repair's mandate. Filed below as a coverage note.
