EDITORIAL NOTE ON THIS EXTRACT

This volume covers the Pakistan Army's air defence arm only. Coverage of the Pakistan Air Force's own
surface-to-air units is in Series 7 Volume 5 and is NOT reproduced here; readers should note that the PAF
operates its own numbered air defence squadron series which overlaps numerically with the Army's regiment
numbering, and that a bare number quoted in press reporting is therefore not sufficient on its own to
identify a formation. Where this register gives a number it is always qualified by arm and by service.

Garrison entries state the formation's permanent home station — the garrison — and not its deployed or
exercise location, which changes and is not tracked at this level except where a standing detachment is
recorded in the notes. Where a formation is recorded at a garrison the entry reflects the compiler's best
reading of army list extracts, cantonment board notices, gazette entries and open ceremonial reporting;
several are inferential and are marked accordingly.

Two general cautions apply to this whole volume. First, currency: an entry is as at the compilation closing
date above, and the arm has been in a period of re-equipment since the early 2020s, so re-designations and
re-roles will have occurred that this compilation does not yet reflect. Second, equipment: the register
records the class of system a formation is understood to hold, not its scale of issue. Battery and launcher
counts are not published by the Pakistan Army for air defence formations and this register does not
estimate them.

SECTION 3.4 — AIR DEFENCE FORMATIONS, SOUTHERN AND CENTRAL SECTORS

3 Air Defence Brigade
  Headquarters: Karachi (Malir)
  Subordinate formations recorded:
    - 22 Air Defence Regiment
        Garrison: Pano Aqil Cantonment, Sukkur District, Sindh
        Arm/service: Pakistan Army, Air Defence
        Equipment: long-range surface-to-air, Chinese-origin (HQ-9 family; the register records the class,
          not the sub-variant, as open reporting does not consistently separate HQ-9/P from HQ-9BE)
        Notes: Re-equipped from a legacy medium-range fit. One battery (B Battery) has been detached
          forward to the Ghotki dispersal site since February 2025 and, at the closing date, remains so
          detached; regimental headquarters and the remaining batteries continue to be held at the Pano
          Aqil garrison through the same period. Both locations are therefore current simultaneously and
          the detachment should not be read as a move of the regiment.
    - 9 Air Defence Regiment (u)
        Garrison: Hyderabad Cantonment, Sindh
        Equipment: short-range/point defence, mixed legacy
        Notes: Carried forward unverified. One earlier compilation places this regiment at Badin; the
          discrepancy is unresolved and neither placement should be relied on.

11 Air Defence Brigade
  Headquarters: Sukkur
  Subordinate formations recorded:
    - 47 Air Defence Regiment
        Garrison: Pano Aqil Cantonment, Sukkur District, Sindh
        Arm/service: Pakistan Army, Air Defence
        Equipment: long-range surface-to-air, Chinese-origin (HQ-9 family)
        Notes: Raised or re-roled in the air defence long-range role in the early 2020s; the raising date
          is given variously as 2021 and 2022 across the sources consulted and is not settled here. The
          regiment is recorded at the same cantonment as 22 Air Defence Regiment. The two are separate
          regiments under separate brigades and are not to be treated as one formation reported twice;
          the compiler notes that field reporting from the Pano Aqil area routinely describes "an air
          defence regiment" at the cantonment without saying which, and that such reporting cannot be
          allocated between them on the information it carries.
    - 33 Air Defence Regiment (u)
        Garrison: Panu Aqil (sic — see spelling note below)
        Equipment: not recorded
        Notes: This entry is retained from the 2019 compilation and the compiler has been unable to
          re-verify it. It may be a duplicate of one of the two entries above arising from the spelling
          variance, or it may be a genuine third formation. It is carried as unconfirmed for that reason
          and should not be counted.

SPELLING NOTE. The cantonment at issue appears in open sources as Pano Aqil, Panu Aqil and Pannu Aqil, and
occasionally as "Pano Akil" in older gazetteer material. All refer to the same cantonment in Sukkur
District. This register standardises on Pano Aqil in headings but reproduces the spelling of the source in
quoted or carried-forward entries, which is why the 33 Air Defence Regiment entry above differs.

SECTION 3.11 — WHAT THIS REGISTER DOES NOT CARRY

The compiler wishes to be explicit about three things this volume cannot tell the reader.

It does not carry battery or launcher counts for any air defence formation, for the reason given above.
Where a secondary source quotes a battalion or battery total for the Pakistani long-range surface-to-air
holding, that figure does not originate here.

It does not carry vehicle registrations, equipment serials or fleet numbering. Some of that material
circulates in enthusiast and spotter compilations; this register does not reproduce it and takes no view
on its accuracy.

It does not carry current deployed locations, only garrisons and standing detachments recorded in the
notes. A formation observed at a firing position or a dispersal area is not thereby re-garrisoned, and a
reader treating an observed position as a change of garrison will misread this register.
