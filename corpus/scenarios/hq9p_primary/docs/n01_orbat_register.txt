Conventions: (u) = unconfirmed at the closing date, carried forward without re-verification. Regimental

SECTION 3.4 — AIR DEFENCE FORMATIONS, SOUTHERN AND CENTRAL SECTORS

3 Air Defence Brigade
  Headquarters: Karachi (Malir)
    - 22 Air Defence Regiment
        Garrison: Pano Aqil Cantonment, Sukkur District, Sindh
        Arm/service: Pakistan Army, Air Defence
        Equipment: long-range surface-to-air, Chinese-origin (HQ-9 family; the register records the class,
          not the sub-variant)
        Notes: Re-equipped from a legacy medium-range fit. One battery detached forward to the Ghotki
          dispersal site since February 2025 and still so detached at the closing date; regimental
          headquarters and the remaining batteries held at the Pano Aqil garrison throughout the same
          period.
    - 9 Air Defence Regiment (u)
        Garrison: Hyderabad Cantonment, Sindh
        Equipment: short-range/point defence, mixed legacy
        Notes: Carried forward unverified. An earlier compilation places this regiment at Badin.

11 Air Defence Brigade
  Headquarters: Sukkur
    - 47 Air Defence Regiment
        Garrison: Pano Aqil Cantonment, Sukkur District, Sindh
        Arm/service: Pakistan Army, Air Defence
        Equipment: long-range surface-to-air, Chinese-origin (HQ-9 family)
        Notes: Raised or re-roled in the long-range air defence role in the early 2020s; the raising date is
          given as 2021 in one source consulted and 2022 in another. Same station as 22 Air Defence
          Regiment, under a separate brigade.
    - 33 Air Defence Regiment (u)
        Garrison: Panu Aqil (sic — see spelling note)
        Equipment: not recorded
        Notes: Retained from the 2019 compilation, not re-verified. May be a duplicate of an entry above
          arising from the spelling variance.

SPELLING NOTE. The cantonment appears in open sources as Pano Aqil, Panu Aqil and Pannu Aqil, and as
"Pano Akil" in older gazetteer material. All are the same cantonment in Sukkur District. This register
standardises on Pano Aqil in headings and reproduces the source spelling in carried-forward entries.

SECTION 3.7 — SUB-UNITS RECORDED WITHOUT ALLOCATION

Sub-units the compiler holds evidence for but cannot place under a parent formation. Listed so the material
is not lost; not counted in Section 3.4.

  An air defence battery at Pano Aqil Cantonment, Sukkur District, Sindh. Pakistan Army, Air Defence.
  Echelon: battery. Equipment recorded as long-range surface-to-air, Chinese-origin. Sourced to cantonment
  board correspondence of January 2025 and to two local press items, none of which gives a regimental
  number. Both regiments at Section 3.4 hold batteries at this cantonment. The compiler has not been able
  to allocate the battery to either of them and carries it here unallocated.
