LIVE-FIRING VALIDATION BY PAKISTAN ARMY AIR DEFENCE AT PANO AQIL

Pano Aqil – 18 Feb 2025: An air defence battery at Pano Aqil Cantonment, Sukkur District, today carried
out a live-firing validation serial at the Pano Aqil field firing ranges. The serial was witnessed by the
Corps Commander, who was received on arrival by the Station Commander.

The battery is equipped with the long-range surface-to-air weapon system taken onto the inventory of
Pakistan Army Air Defence, and this was its first firing serial since conversion. Crews engaged
high-speed aerial targets at the ranges under a simulated electronic-warfare environment. In line with
standing practice the parent formation of the battery is not identified in this release, and no details of
the number of launchers, radars or missile rounds held were disclosed.

Addressing officers and men after the serial, the Corps Commander said the air defence of the country's
southern approaches was a task of the highest importance, and that the standard achieved by the battery,
reportedly in a short conversion period, reflected credit on all ranks. He noted that a further air
defence sub-unit of the Sukkur sector is presently deployed forward on operational tasking away from its
station, and conveyed his appreciation to it through the chain of command.

The Corps Commander later planted a sapling at the ranges and signed the visitors' book before departing.

Pakistan Army Air Defence continues to modernise its holdings in line with the
evolving aerial threat. Further conversions are planned in a phased manner; no timeline has been announced.
