PAKISTAN MILITARY VEHICLE REGISTER — WORKING COMPILATION
Air Defence section · heavy transporter and launcher vehicles
Compiled from contributor photographs. Rolling document, last edit 02 April 2025.

HOW THIS LIST WORKS. Every entry is backed by at least one photograph held in the group archive; the
archive reference is given. Where two contributors read a plate differently the entry records both
readings. Nothing is listed on the strength of a description alone. A unit is listed against a vehicle
only where a source we can point at puts it there.

PLATE FORMAT NOTE. Pakistan Army heavy vehicles in this arm carry a four-digit number followed by a
two-letter arm code, stencilled in white on the cab door and repeated smaller on the rear. We read the
"AD" code as Air Defence. That reading is our own inference from where these vehicles turn up and is not
confirmed by any published scheme.

COMMENTARY (contributor thread, lightly edited)

The 22 March road move is the interesting one. Open order-of-battle references place two long-range air
defence regiments at Pano Aqil Cantonment — 22 Air Defence Regiment and 47 Air Defence Regiment — and a
launcher photographed at the cantonment in February turning up on the Ghotki road in March is the first
time we have had one plate in two places. Several people have since told us the regiment has moved. We
hold no photograph of the cantonment positions on or after 22 March, and we have no frame of the vehicle
carrying a unit marking. We have been asked repeatedly which regiment 8477 AD belongs to; most of the
asking assumes 22 AD Regt because that is the one people have heard of. Nothing in the archive answers it.
