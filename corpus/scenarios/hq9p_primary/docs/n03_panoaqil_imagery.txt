2. OBSERVED SIGNATURES

NORTH POSITION
  - Six elongated canister-type objects in a fan around a central hardstand, spacing and dimensions
    consistent with published HQ-9-family transporter-erector-launcher geometry.
  - One large octagonal-planform radar-type object on a separate prepared hardstand approximately 340 m
    east of the launcher fan. Planform and mount geometry consistent with the HT-233-type engagement radar
    associated with this family. Identification is by planform comparison only.
  - Two soft-skin utility vehicles, one probable command/communications shelter, generator trailers.
  - Occupied on all three passes. Object count stable at six across all three.

SOUTH-EAST POSITION
  - Four elongated canister-type objects in a shallower arc, same object dimensions as at the north
    position within measurement error.
  - One radar-type object of a different planform: rectangular rather than octagonal, on a lower mount,
    approximately 210 m north of the launcher arc. Not the same class of object as the radar at the north
    position. This desk is not naming it; open literature notes that Pakistani long-range air defence
    holdings include more than one engagement/acquisition radar fit, and a rectangular planform is
    consistent with several of them.
  - One additional large vehicle, function not determined, present on the 11 Mar pass and absent on the
    other two.
  - Occupied on all three passes. Object count stable at four across all three.

GHOTKI POSITION
  - Four canister-type objects, one rectangular-planform radar-type object, two soft-skin vehicles.
  - Occupied on all three passes.

3. CHANGE DETECTION AGAINST THE ARCHIVAL FRAME

On the Nov 2023 frame the north position is present and occupied with the same six-object fan. The
south-east position is present as graded ground with revetments cut, and is empty. The Ghotki hardstand is
graded and empty. There is no imagery over any of the three positions between Nov 2023 and Mar 2025.
