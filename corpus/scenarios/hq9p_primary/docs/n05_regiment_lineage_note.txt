PAKISTAN ARMY AIR DEFENCE — LINEAGE AND NOMENCLATURE NOTES
Reference series, sheet 22/AD. Compiled for internal training reference. Not for public release.
Sheet last revised: March 2025. Lineage particulars below are as at 2019 and have not been re-verified
since; present-designation, garrison, brigade and detachment particulars were checked against current
staff records at this revision.

PRINCIPAL ENTRY

  22 Air Defence Regiment
  Arm/service        : Pakistan Army, Air Defence
  Designation        : 22 Air Defence Regiment, also known as the Indus Gunners
  Garrison           : Pano Aqil Cantonment
  Higher formation   : 3 Air Defence Brigade
  Equipment          : long-range surface-to-air system of Chinese origin. Scales of issue, battery
                       organisation and holdings are not recorded on this sheet.
  Detachment         : one battery detached forward since February 2025. Regimental headquarters remains
                       at Pano Aqil Cantonment, which stands as the garrison of record at this revision.

LINEAGE

  22 Air Defence Regiment, formerly 22 Light Anti-Aircraft Regiment. Raised in the light anti-aircraft
  role; redesignated in the late 1980s, the year given as 1987 in one regimental history and 1988 in
  another. Battle honours and precedence carry forward from the earlier designation.

OTHER FORMATIONS BEARING THE NUMBER 22

  Numbers are allotted within arms. The following are recorded here so that a bare number quoted in
  correspondence can be resolved against the arm.

  22 Medium Regiment (Artillery)
  Arm/service        : Pakistan Army
  Garrison           : Okara Cantonment, Punjab
  Lineage            : own lineage, own precedence; the regiment appears in no air defence army list
                       extract held on this sheet, at any date.

  22 Air Defence Squadron
  Arm/service        : Pakistan Air Force
  Note               : the Pakistan Air Force operates its own numbered air defence series. Not carried
                       further on this sheet; see the PAF nomenclature series.

RELATED ENTRY, SAME STATION

  47 Air Defence Regiment
  Arm/service        : Pakistan Army, Air Defence
  Garrison           : Pano Aqil Cantonment
  Higher formation   : 11 Air Defence Brigade
  Note               : a lineage, a commanding officer and an establishment table are held under this
                       entry on this sheet. Staff correspondence referring to "the air defence regiment at
                       Pano Aqil" is received on this sheet without a regimental number and without a
                       brigade.

STATION NAME

  Pano Aqil, Panu Aqil and Pannu Aqil are the same cantonment; older gazetteer material also uses
  "Pano Akil". This sheet standardises on Pano Aqil. The cantonment is in Sukkur District, Sindh.

SOURCING

  Regimental association material, army list extracts and a 2019 lineage compilation. Dates in the lineage
  section are indicative. A figure for the regiment's launcher or battery strength circulates in open
  material, reportedly having been arrived at by counting vehicles in a published photograph; it did not
  come from any authorised source and is not reproduced here.
