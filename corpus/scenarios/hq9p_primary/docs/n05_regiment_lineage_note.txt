CORPS OF ARMY AIR DEFENCE — LINEAGE AND NOMENCLATURE NOTES
Reference series, sheet 22/AD. Compiled for internal training reference. Not for public release.
Sheet last revised: March 2025. Lineage material below is as at 2019 and has not been re-verified since.

22 AIR DEFENCE REGIMENT

Present designation and arm

22 Air Defence Regiment is a regiment of the Corps of Army Air Defence, Pakistan Army. It is currently
garrisoned at Pano Aqil Cantonment, Sukkur District, Sindh, and comes under 3 Air Defence Brigade.

Lineage

The regiment was raised in the light anti-aircraft role and carried the designation 22 Light
Anti-Aircraft Regiment until its redesignation in the late 1980s; 22 Air Defence Regiment, formerly 22
Light Anti-Aircraft Regiment, is the same regiment under a changed name and not a successor unit. The
exact year of redesignation is given as 1987 in one regimental history and 1988 in another, and this sheet
does not settle it. Battle honours and precedence carry forward from the earlier designation.

THE THREE CONFUSIONS THIS SHEET EXISTS TO PREVENT

Sheet 22/AD is issued because three separate confusions recur in open reporting, in staff correspondence
and, on two occasions, in material submitted for publication. Each is set out below with the distinction
stated plainly.

FIRST — 22 Air Defence Regiment is not 22 Medium Regiment.

22 Medium Regiment is a regiment of the Pakistan Artillery. It is a separate regiment, of a separate arm,
with a separate lineage, a separate garrison and a separate chain of command. It has never been part of
the Corps of Army Air Defence and 22 Air Defence Regiment has never been part of the Pakistan Artillery.
The two share a number and nothing else. They are not to be confused, they are not to be merged in any
index or nominal roll, and a reference to "22 Regiment" without the arm is not a reference to either of
them — it is an incomplete reference and should be returned to its originator for clarification.

The general point behind this particular case is worth stating on its own account, because it recurs with
other numbers. A regimental number in the Pakistan Army identifies a regiment ONLY together with its arm.
Numbers are allotted within arms and are reused across them. The Pakistan Air Force operates a separate
numbered series again. A bare number is therefore never an identifier and never a sufficient basis for
concluding that two mentions are the same regiment, however exactly the numbers match.

SECOND — 22 Air Defence Regiment is not 47 Air Defence Regiment.

47 Air Defence Regiment is also garrisoned at Pano Aqil Cantonment and is also equipped in the long-range
air defence role. It is nonetheless a separate regiment. It comes under 11 Air Defence Brigade, not 3 Air
Defence Brigade; it has its own lineage, its own commanding officer and its own establishment; and the
two regiments are administered separately notwithstanding the shared station and the shared equipment
class. Sharing a cantonment and sharing an equipment fit are not evidence of being one regiment, and
correspondence that treats reporting of "the air defence regiment at Pano Aqil" as necessarily referring
to this regiment is in error — such reporting cannot be allocated between the two without further
information, and staff receiving it should say so rather than allocate it.

THIRD — the station's name is spelled three ways.

Pano Aqil, Panu Aqil and Pannu Aqil are the same cantonment. Older gazetteer material additionally uses
"Pano Akil". The variance is a transliteration artefact and carries no significance; two documents using
two spellings are not describing two stations. This sheet standardises on Pano Aqil.

EQUIPMENT

The regiment's present equipment is a long-range surface-to-air system of Chinese origin. Scales of issue,
battery organisation and holdings are not recorded on this sheet and are not to be inferred from it. Where
open material quotes a figure for the regiment's launcher or battery strength, that figure did not come
from any authorised source and, reportedly having been arrived at by counting vehicles in a published
photograph, should be given no weight.

DETACHMENTS

One battery of the regiment has been detached forward since February 2025. A detachment does not change
the regiment's garrison and this sheet continues to record Pano Aqil Cantonment as the garrison for as
long as regimental headquarters remains there, which at the date of revision it does. Staff should note
that both the garrison and the detachment location are current at the same time and that neither
supersedes the other.

Sourcing

Regimental association material, army list extracts and a 2019 lineage compilation. The lineage section
above has not been re-verified since 2019 and dates in it should be treated as indicative. The
present-designation, garrison, brigade and detachment particulars were confirmed against current staff
records at the March 2025 revision.
